How to Vote

Voting can feel unfamiliar the first time. These steps can help you prepare.

Your Voting Checklist

  1. Confirm that you are registered.
  2. Find out which elections are coming up.
  3. Review your ballot and research your choices.
  4. Check available voting methods and deadlines.
  5. Follow the instructions for casting your ballot.
