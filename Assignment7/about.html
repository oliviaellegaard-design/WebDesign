About & Contact

Our Mission

Voter Ready is an independent student educational website designed to help first-time voters understand the voting process.

Our goal is to offer clear, approachable, nonpartisan information and direct visitors to official election resources.

Contact

For questions or feedback, please contact the project creator through the contact method provided by your class or organization.

Disclaimer: Voter Ready is not an official government website.
