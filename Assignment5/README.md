# Assignment 4 - README Questions

## Visual Appeal

### Which icons did you ultimately use and for what purposes?

I used several travel-themed icons throughout my website. I used a home icon for the Home page, an airplane for the Destinations page, a food icon for the Food page, a star for the Ratings page, an About icon for the About page, and an information icon for the Info page. I also used custom pink browser tab icons so each page matches the overall travel theme of my website.

### Which consistent colors, values, lines, and/or shapes did you utilize and why?

I used a consistent pink color palette throughout my website, including light pink, medium pink, white, and darker pink accents. I used rounded corners, borders, and card shapes to make the website feel friendly and organized. I also used consistent spacing and centered layouts so the different pages feel connected and easy to follow.

### Which Google Fonts did you use and for what purposes?

I used **Playfair Display** for my headings because it gives the titles a more decorative and elegant appearance. I used **Poppins** for body text, navigation, buttons, forms, and tables because it is clean and easy to read.

## Structure & Organization

### What information does your table organize, and why was a table appropriate?

My table organizes my travel ratings for Copenhagen, Japan, and Mexico. It compares each destination based on overall rating, food rating, and accessibility. A table was appropriate because it allows visitors to quickly compare the same categories across multiple destinations without having to read every paragraph.

### What specific style choices helped you structure and organize your form?

I placed the form inside a pink card so it matches the rest of my website. I used labels for each form field, consistent spacing, rounded borders, and centered inputs. I also used text inputs, a dropdown menu, a text area, and a styled submit button to keep the form organized and easy to understand.

## Design Goals

### What is the goal of your form, and how did it support your site's goals?

The goal of my form is to allow visitors to recommend a destination that I should travel to next. This supports the goal of my travel website because it encourages visitors to interact with the site and think about travel destinations, cultures, and places they enjoy.

### How does the gallery support your website's goals?

My gallery on the About Me page shows different travel photos from Copenhagen, Japan, and Mexico. The gallery supports the goal of my website by giving visitors a visual look at the destinations I discuss throughout the site. The photos also make the website more personal and visually interesting.

#### What pattern did you use: uniform, alternating, or something else, and why?

I used a uniform gallery pattern with multiple columns. I used `display: flex;` to organize the different travel photos and create consistent spacing between them. I chose a uniform layout because it keeps the gallery organized and makes the different destinations easy to view.
